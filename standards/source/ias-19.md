## Paragraph 67

An entity shall use the projected unit credit method to determine the present
value of its defined benefit obligations and the related current service cost
and, where applicable, past service cost.

## Paragraph 68

The projected unit credit method (sometimes known as the accrued benefit method
pro-rated on service or as the benefit/years of service method) sees each period
of service as giving rise to an additional unit of benefit entitlement (see
paragraphs 70–74) and measures each unit separately to build up the final
obligation (see paragraphs 75–98).
