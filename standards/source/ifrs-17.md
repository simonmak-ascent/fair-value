## Paragraph 32

The fulfilment cash flows comprise:

(a) estimates of future cash flows;

(b) an adjustment to reflect the time value of money and the financial risks
related to the future cash flows, to the extent that the financial risks are not
included in the estimates of future cash flows; and

(c) a risk adjustment for non-financial risk.
